Guidance for New Users

Home | About | Contact | Book Now |

Step 1: Install the App

Download the Rapido application from your mobile app store and install it on your smartphone. Download Here


Step 2: Register Your Account

Create an account using your mobile number and verify it using the OTP sent to your phone.


Step 3: Enable Location

Allow the application to access your current location for accurate pickup and drop locations.


Step 4: Enter Your Destination

Type your destination and select the preferred ride option.


Step 5: Confirm Your Booking

Review the fare estimate, pickup point, and destination before confirming the ride.


Step 6: Meet Your Captain

Once a captain accepts your request, you can view the driver's name, phone number, vehicle details, and live location.

Step 7: Complete Your Ride

After reaching your destination, pay using your preferred payment method and provide a rating for your ride experience.

Safety Tips

  • Verify the vehicle number before boarding.
  • Match the captain's name with the app details.
  • Wear a helmet during bike rides.
  • Share your live trip with family or friends.
  • Follow traffic safety rules.
  • Contact customer support if you face any issues.

Thank You

Thank you for visiting the Rapido User Guide.

We hope this webpage helps new users understand how to use Rapido safely and efficiently. Have a safe and comfortable journey!
