Welcome

Welcome to Rapido User Guide, a simple guide created to help new users understand how to use the Rapido app. This webpage explains the basic features of Rapido, how to book a ride, and important safety tips for a smooth travel experience.Whether you are traveling to work, college, or any nearby destination, Rapido offers a quick and convenient way to commute.

Rapido is an online bike taxi and cab booking platform that connects passengers with nearby riders through a mobile application. It provides affordable, fast, and convenient transportation for daily travel.Users can choose from different ride options based on their travel needs and can track their ride in real time using the app.


About Rapido

Rapido was founded in 2015 with the aim of making short-distance travel simple and affordable. Today, it operates in many cities across India and serves millions of customers every day.

Services Offered

  • Bike Taxi
  • Auto Rickshaw
  • Cab Service
  • Parcel Delivery (available in selected cities)

Benefits of Using Rapido
