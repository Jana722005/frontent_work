Introduction

Bengaluru, also known as Bangalore, is the capital city of the Indian state of Karnataka. It is widely recognized as the "Silicon Valley of India" due to its thriving IT industry and vibrant startup ecosystem. The city is home to numerous multinational technology companies, research institutions, and innovation centers. Bengaluru's favorable climate, cosmopolitan culture, and skilled workforce make it an attractive destination for businesses and professionals in the technology sector.
